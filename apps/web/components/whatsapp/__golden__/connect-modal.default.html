2 Ways to Setup WhatsApp API Number

You can connect your number in two ways. Here's how they differ.

WA Business App Number

Requirements Before Connecting

•A number registered on WhatsApp Business App version 2.24.4+

•GST Certificate or Active Website needed for verification

Number

•No new number needed

•Use your existing WhatsApp Business App number

App Usage

•Continue using WhatsApp Business App alongside WBMSG

•Messages sync between WBMSG & app

Broadcasts

•Slower Broadcast Speeds

•Broadcast to 10,000 contacts could take an hour to send

Chat Automations

•Possible to use Chatbots & AI Agent for customer replies

Groups, Status & Calling

•Groups, Status, and Calling available on WA Business App

•WA Calling not possible from WBMSG

Display Name

•Display name depends on contact saving

•Customers see your name only if they saved your number

New Number

Requirements Before Connecting

•Fresh number not on WA Personal/Business

•Must be able to receive OTP via call or SMS

•GST Certificate or Active Website needed for verification

Number

•Requires a fresh phone number

•Cannot be already registered on WhatsApp

App Usage

•Cannot use WhatsApp Business/Personal app

•Fully API-based — manage everything inside WBMSG

Broadcasts

•Faster Broadcast Speeds

•Broadcast to 10,000 contacts in just a few minutes

Chat Automations

•Possible to use Chatbots & AI Agent for customer replies

Groups, Status & Calling

•Groups & Status sharing won't be available

•Dedicated API number only — no WA app access

Display Name

•Verified business name after verification

•Customers see your business name even without saving your number
